Attendance Security (Geofencing)

Checking live kiosk status...
Only allow employees to time in/out from the office
Applies to employees scanning their own QR with their own phone. Off by default so it never blocks attendance until you set it up.
Stand at the office (where employees will scan from), then tap this on your own phone/laptop.
Phone GPS is typically only accurate to ~10-30m outdoors and can be much worse indoors - a 5m radius will likely reject employees standing right at the office. 30-50m is a safer default; the app also auto-adds a small buffer based on each phone's reported GPS accuracy.
This check runs on the server, not just in the browser, so it can't be bypassed by editing the page. It stops casual "clock in from home" attempts, but a phone with GPS-spoofing tools enabled could still fake a location - for stronger guarantees, consider pairing this with a physical NFC tag or a QR code that only displays at the office.
