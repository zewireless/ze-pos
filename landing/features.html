Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need special hardware?

No. ZE-POS runs in any modern browser and installs as a PWA on tablets and phones. Pair a thermal printer for receipts if you like.

How do I pay?

Monthly via GCash, Maya, or bank transfer. Submit a claim in Billing and the admin activates it. PayMongo checkout is coming soon.

Can cashiers have their own login?

Yes. Each person gets their own secure Supabase account via a 7-day invite code. You never see their password.

What if my internet drops?

ZE-POS keeps an offline outbox and retries writes when you're back online, so a brief dropout won't stop a sale.

Is my data safe?

Row-level security isolates every business and store. A paywall is enforced in the database, and all sensitive changes are audited.
