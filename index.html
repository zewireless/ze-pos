How ZE-POS works

1

Sign up & build your menu

Create your business, drop in categories and items with photos and prices. A starter menu is ready on day one.

2

Invite your team

Send cashiers a 7-day invite code. Set their schedules so the register only opens when they're on the floor.

3

Sell & track live

Start a shift, take dine-in / takeaway / delivery orders, print receipts, and watch sales update in real time.

4

Close, pay & grow

Reconcile the till, run payroll from clocked hours, and read reports to make smarter calls next week.

Everything you need — one sidebar away
